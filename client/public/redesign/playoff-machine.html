How to read this edition

This is the Week 12 outlook, using final Week 11 standings and the Week 12–14 schedule. It is a snapshot, not a live calculator. All remaining games are assumed to have a winner. A tied game or corrected result requires a new edition.

For Gents, Beaners & Husseins, and Rebirth, every one of the 262,144 remaining win/loss combinations was counted equally. Scenario percentages are not predicted odds. A range separates qualification without a tiebreaker from qualification with a favorable points-for tiebreaker. An exact season-points tie is unresolved.

STS uses 4 points per win and 3 per top-six scoring week. Its status and points thresholds were calculated separately. Possible STS paths need other results that are not fully enumerated; no percentage is shown.
